## Question

How does a sequence run while the student is playing?

Settle: auto-advance after the set number of bars, count-in, looping the whole
sequence, manual next and previous, what the playhead highlights, how tempo is
per step or per sequence, and how the student starts, pauses, and restarts
without losing their place.

Prototype the transition between two steps and the count-in, at practice tempo.
The resolution names the behaviour and links the prototype.
